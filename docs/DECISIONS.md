# Decisions and deviations from Spec v1.0

Every place where the implementation departs from, resolves, or adds to the v1.0 spec (consolidated in [SPEC_v2.md](SPEC_v2.md#summary-of-changes-from-v10)).
IDs are referenced from code comments and configs. Status: **resolved** = a §19 open item settled (may be revisited),
**deviation** = differs from v1.0 text, **addition** = not in v1.0.

| ID | Type | Decision | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | deviation | **Python 3.12**, MetaDrive installed from GitHub commit `fe54b03` (still reports 0.4.3) instead of PyPI 0.4.3 / Python 3.10–3.11. | PyPI 0.4.3 caps Python < 3.12 and pins panda3d 1.10.13 (no cp312 wheels); GitHub `main` lifts both. Team preference to stay on 3.12. | `pyproject.toml` |
| D-02 | resolved / deviation | **Map**: explicit block list (`PG_MAP_FILE`): first block 50 m + 4 × 240 m straights = **1010 m**, 3 lanes × 3.5 m. **One-way road** (no oncoming carriageway) via MetaDrive's `remove_negative_lanes`. | MetaDrive samples straight-block lengths in [40, 80] m by default; spec wants a fixed ~1 km route. One-way requested in review (the oncoming side was unusable scenery). | `simulator/metadrive_env.py` (`build_block_config`, `OneWayPGMap`), `base.yaml: sim.map` |
| D-03 | resolved | **Dynamics pinning** through MetaDrive's `VaryingDynamicsEnv` with equal min/max `random_dynamics` ranges. | Uses MetaDrive's own mechanism (no source changes) and makes reach goal 1 (dynamics mismatch) a config change. | `simulator/metadrive_env.py` |
| D-04 | resolved | **M2**: N2 = 5 (0.5 s), T_traj = 2 s, lateral margin 1 m. **M3**: fixed decision period N3 = 10 (1 s), Δs ∈ [5, 40] m, margin 1 m. | §19 defaults agreed in design review. | `method/m2.yaml`, `method/m3.yaml` |
| D-05 | resolved | **Frenet conventions**: reference line = centreline of MetaDrive lane 0 (leftmost forward lane); `d` positive to the left; lanes at d = 0, −3.5, −7.0. Ego observation uses `d` relative to the road centre ÷ road half-width. | Spec left the sign convention to the code. | `simulator/road.py`, `rl/env/core.py` |
| D-06 | resolved / addition | **Safe-set infeasibility**: drop the infeasible waypoint and all later ones, charge 10 m projection distance each; none left → hold the current lane. **Reachability** = min spacing 2 m, reach ≤ v_cap·10 s, lateral shift ≤ min(0.16·Δs, a_lat·Δs²/(5.77 v²)) with a_lat = 3 m/s² (exact peak lateral accel of the quintic spline segments). | §19 open item; the lateral-accel form was added after over-aggressive projected lane changes drove off-road. | `rl/methods/m3_safe_set.py` |
| D-07 | addition | **Cadences in control steps**: training budget, evaluation and checkpoint frequency are counted in control steps (simulated time), not RL decisions. Evaluation also at step 0. Best checkpoint = highest success rate, ties broken by return. | Methods with longer decision periods are compared at equal simulated experience. | `rl/callbacks.py` |
| D-08 | resolved | **Traffic**: per-vehicle IDM cruise speed; lead (XL) never changes lanes; background lane changes **off** (keeps the lead the front-most car in its lane in `freeway_pass`). Stopped vehicles (target speed < 1 km/h) hold full brake (stock IDM divides by the target speed). | Spec §4.4; the lane-change choice keeps the scenario well-defined. | `simulator/metadrive_env.py`, `scenario/*.yaml` |
| D-09 | deviation | **BEV**: MetaDrive `TopDownMultiChannel`, window side **100 m** (MetaDrive's `distance` is the window side length, so ±50 m = R), **5 channels** (road, past positions, 3 traffic frames — MetaDrive's multi-channel obs does not include the navigation channel), delivered **channels-first (5, 84, 84)**. | SB3's NatureCNN reads channels from `shape[0]` when `normalize_images=False`. | `rl/env/core.py`, `base.yaml: observation` |
| D-10 | addition (pitfall) | **Out-of-road is geometric** (centre outside the road in Frenet coordinates, or beyond the route ends) + MetaDrive's continuous-line/sidewalk contacts. Traffic is removed only when geometrically off-road. | MetaDrive's `on_lane` is a single vertical raycast against lane meshes that misses at seams (~0.1 % of road points beyond x ≈ 150 m) → spurious mid-lane off-road terminations and silently deleted traffic. | `simulator/metadrive_env.py` (`geometric_off_road`) |
| D-11 | addition (pitfall) | `force_destroy: true` (objects destroyed on reset, not reused). | Reused objects carry ~1e-5 m state noise; closed loops amplify it to cm. With it, re-simulation is bit-exact → replay by re-simulation works. | `base.yaml: sim.metadrive_overrides` |
| D-12 | resolved | **Actuation mapping** a → throttle_brake: static lookup table measured open-loop (positive throttle linear 2.909 m/s² per unit; brake strongly nonlinear, ≈ −8 m/s² at −0.3, −15.8 at −1). **MPC model** adds a 0.1 s first-order steering lag and a lateral-acceleration bound (4 m/s²). | No sys-ID (§19), but two gains cannot represent the brake curve; the lag and bound were measured/needed for model fidelity. | `base.yaml: actuation`, `rl/methods/common.py`, `rl/methods/m3_mpc.py` |
| D-13 | deviation | **M3 collision avoidance = hard convex corridor** (4 half-spaces per stage on the ego centre, cars inflated by the ego footprint), built around an initial trajectory and rebuilt every 10 control steps / each new RL reference; boxes road-aligned, rotated to the trajectory while lane-changing. The MPC speed cost tracks the corridor trajectory's speed (= global target unless slowing for a blocking car). No slack variables. | v1.0 said soft constraints with penalised slack; soft formulations (super-ellipse, then per-stage half-planes) produced "drive through the obstacle" optima and brake/swerve flip-flopping. Corridor design specified in review. | `rl/methods/m3_corridor.py`, `rl/methods/m3_mpc.py`, `method/m3.yaml: mpc.corridor` |
| D-14 | resolved | **Reward/termination details**: R0 terminal priority success > off_road > collision (MetaDrive's order); episode termination *reason* priority success > collision > off_road > timeout. `projection_penalty` weight 0 in R0. Example variants `r1_sparse`, `r2_dense_comfort` (untuned). | Spec §9.3; reasons favour reporting a crash over the off-road it may cause. | `rl/env/rewards.py`, `rl/eval/metrics.py` |
| D-15 | resolved | **Discount** scaled per method (γ = 0.99^(T_decision/0.1 s)); GAE λ **not** scaled. | Spec §8 specifies γ only. | `rl/env/factory.py` |
| D-16 | resolved | **M2 PID**: MetaDrive LaneChangePolicy lateral gains; speed PID (0.6, 0.02, 0) + feed-forward of the reference acceleration through the actuation table; replans start from the previous reference's lateral rate/accel (continuity). | §7.2 leaves details open. Gains are untuned. | `rl/methods/m2_pid.py`, `method/m2.yaml` |
| D-17 | addition | **Scenario `freeway_dense`**: 22 background cars along the whole route, all lanes, 10 m min spacing, all traffic (incl. lead) at 30 km/h; ego spawns at 40–70 km/h, target 70 km/h. `freeway_pass` (spec scenario) unchanged. | Requested in review for aggressive driving. | `scenario/freeway_dense.yaml` |
| D-18 | deviation | **Training on Great Lakes** (GPU or CPU partitions); local machine for development only. CUDA used automatically for PPO updates; simulation and MPC stay on CPU. | Spec targeted laptop-only with cluster as fallback; team decision. | `slurm/`, [GREAT_LAKES.md](GREAT_LAKES.md) |
| D-19 | addition | **Metrics approximations**: min gap and TTC use Frenet axis-aligned boxes (exact for aligned headings on the straight road; under-reports gap when the ego is strongly yawed). | Cheap per step; adequate for this map. | `simulator/state.py` |
| D-20 | note | The lead can spawn up to ~65 m ahead (gap up to 60 m bumper-to-bumper), i.e. initially outside R = 50 m and absent from the ObjectList until the ego closes in. | Consequence of spec ranges; left as is. | `scenario/freeway_pass.yaml` |
| D-21 | addition | Package name `handoff`; pytest + ruff (line length 120); YAML overrides parse `1e-4`-style numbers as floats. | Tooling left to implementation (§15). | `pyproject.toml`, `config.py` |
